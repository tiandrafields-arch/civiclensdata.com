The methodology

Human-designed. AI-accelerated.

Every analysis runs inside a methodology built from equity frameworks, domain expertise, and community translation standards. AI is the engine. It is never the driver.

Human layer

The framework that governs

  • Equity frameworks developed through a decade of policy analysis and community advocacy
  • Legal context mapping across statutes, regulations, and case law triggers
  • Community translation standards written for real people, not policy insiders
  • Human review of every output before publication
AI layer

The engine that executes

  • Automated ingestion and normalization of public government datasets
  • Pattern detection, anomaly flagging, and longitudinal trend analysis at machine speed
  • Plain-language summarization at three reading levels simultaneously
  • One-click generation of advocacy briefs, legal memoranda, and media summaries
Full AI transparency disclosure ships with every analysis — what the machine did, what the human decided, and how to verify both.
The infrastructure

Spatial analysis, built to deploy.

Interactive data dashboards powered by a proven geospatial and automation stack — validated through live legislative battles in Louisiana.

Analysis engine

Python + QGIS

Spatial joins at the census tract, district, and precinct level. Certified centroid-in-polygon methodology with demographic disaggregation across race, income, age, and geography.

Delivery layer

HTML + CSS Dashboards

Lightweight, interactive, publicly hosted impact dashboards that load on any device — no login, no paywall, no data science degree required.

Data operations

Airtable Pipeline

Structured data operations connecting ingestion to publication, with version history and methodology documentation preserved as a public evidence record.
